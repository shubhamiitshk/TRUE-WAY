### IN-DEPTH CAPITALIST TEARDOWN PER ROUTE

### Route A: Direct B2C Sales (The App Store Mirage)
* **The Reality:** Selling regional heritage packs via In-App Purchases (IAP) at ₹149–₹299 ($1.99–$3.99) directly to retail consumers in India is a proven graveyard for venture capital.
* **The Math:** Domestic Indian consumer willingness-to-pay for standalone mobile software is notoriously low. Acquiring an app install via Meta/Google ads in the travel category costs ₹45–₹120. Conversion from free download to paid regional pack hovers at 2.5%–4.0%. **Blended CAC per paying customer exceeds ₹1,100, against an LTV of ₹240.**
* **The Capitalist Verdict:** **ROUTE A CANNOT BE THE PRIMARY ENGINE.** It serves exclusively as the organic download terminal and fulfillment engine for vouchers issued through B2B and B2B2C channels. We spend **$0 on paid consumer user acquisition**.

### Route B: B2B2C OTA & Experiences Distribution (The Cashflow Arbitrage)
* **The Reality:** As established by `@ota-analyst` and `@experiences-analyst`, platforms like Viator, Klook, MakeMyTrip, and EaseMyTrip spend millions acquiring heritage travelers who are completely unmonetized at the monument.
* **The Math:** TrueWay lists digital pass SKUs on Viator/Klook ($6.99–$12.99) and checkout ancillaries on MMT/EMT (₹149–₹199). 
  * OTA takes 20%–25% commission.
  * TrueWay captures **75% net revenue ($5.24 to $9.74 per pass)**.
  * Customer Acquisition Cost is **$0 upfront** (the OTA takes its cut purely on successful delivery).
  * TrueWay inference cost is **$0.00** (runs on client MobileCLIP-S0 edge runtime).
* **The Policy Arbitrage:** We bypass GetYourGuide’s standalone audio ban using the **"Entry Plus" Trojan Horse** (bundling our digital guide with official ASI entrance tickets via ground concessionaires for a $19.99 package).
* **The Capitalist Verdict:** **ROUTE B IS THE IMMEDIATE CASHFLOW & VOLUME ENGINE.** It generates immediate gross margin within 14 days of listing, validates product-market fit, and builds the verified review moat.

### Route C: High-Ticket Enterprise B2B Licensing (The Enterprise Moat)
* **The Reality:** Enterprise buyers—such as the **IRCTC Buddhist Circuit Tourist Train**, State Tourism Boards (**BSTDC Bihar, UP Tourism, Rajasthan Tourism**), and luxury inbound tour operators (**Abercrombie & Kent, Intrepid Travel, Micato Safaris**)—operate high-margin tours ($2,500–$8,000 per passenger) where visitor dissatisfaction over scammy local guides or lack of scholarly interpretation directly damages their premium brand.
* **The Math:**
  * **IRCTC Luxury Buddhist Circuit Train:** 8-day all-inclusive pilgrimage train running from Delhi through Bodh Gaya, Nalanda, Rajgir, Varanasi, Sarnath, Kushinagar, and Lumbini. Ticket prices: $1,200 to $1,800 per passenger. 
  * TrueWay offers a white-labeled "IRCTC Heritage Companion" enterprise license at **₹25 Lakhs (~$30,000 USD) annual recurring fee**, or **₹750 per passenger onboarding fee**.
  * **State Tourism Boards (BSTDC):** Smart Tourism corridor deployment contract: **₹35 Lakhs–₹50 Lakhs ($42k–$60k) pilot** for Bodh Gaya/Nalanda/Rajgir, including physical signage QR co-branding and scholar dashboard access.
* **The Capitalist Verdict:** **ROUTE C IS THE HIGH-MARGIN VALUATION ANCHOR.** 3 to 5 enterprise accounts yield ₹1.5 Cr–₹2.5 Cr in ARR with near-100% renewal retention and massive institutional defensibility.

### Route D: IP / SDK & Content Syndication (The Deep Moat)
* **The Reality:** Licensing our compiled on-device MobileCLIP-S0 quantized model (`.tflite` / ONNX INT8, 15MB) or our Pali/Buddhist academic citation graph to third-party travel tech apps or camera OEMs (Samsung India, Xiaomi).
* **The Risk:** High technical integration friction, long enterprise procurement timelines (9–18 months), and catastrophic risk of IP reverse-engineering or unauthorized model distillation.
* **The Capitalist Verdict:** **ROUTE D IS A PHASE 3 STRATEGIC HARVEST.** Do not offer raw SDKs or weights in Year 1. Only license headless containerized runtime APIs to trusted institutional players after establishing dominant ground market share.

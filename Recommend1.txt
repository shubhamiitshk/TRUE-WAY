## II. Technology Recommendations by Domain

### A. Core Recognition & Retrieval Architecture

**Current plan (§7.3) benchmarks four separate models. Consolidate to a two-tier hybrid:**

| Tier | Recommended Technology | Role | Why This Over Alternatives |
|------|----------------------|------|---------------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Always On)** | MediaPipe Image Embedder with MobileNet-v3 backbone | Primary landmark retrieval via cosine similarity | Purpose-built for Android, runs on CPU/NPU, 10–50ms inference, no model fragmentation. EmbeddingGemma 2 (740M, 768-dim) is overkill for retrieval when you have only 50–70 POIs.【turn4search11】【turn4search12】 |
| **Tier 2 (Optional, Device-Dependent)** | Gemma 4 E2B (on-device multimodal) | Visual verification, architectural Q&A, offline narration, contextual explanation | Single model handles image understanding + text generation + audio comprehension. The E2B variant fits in 2GB RAM, enabling flagship-tier conversational heritage experiences without cloud.【turn4search1】【turn4search2】 |
| **Tier 3 (P2 Differentiator)** | Niantic Spatial VPS2 SDK | Centimeter-accurate AR placement, monument relocalization | Niantic's Large Geospatial Model is trained on billions of real-world scans. Their Belvedere Museum deployment proves cultural-site viability. This is your AR timeline anchor, not ARCore Geospatial.【turn3search9】【turn3search12】 |

**Critical update to §7.4 pipeline:** Use Gemma 4 E2B's vision encoder as your secondary feature extractor alongside the MediaPipe embedding. Two independent embedding spaces create a more robust open-set decision than a single encoder. Fuse scores via learned calibration (as your §7.5 correctly specifies).

### B. On-Device Retrieval & Knowledge Storage

**Recommendation: Replace your planned flat vector matrix with SQLite + the `sqlite-vec` extension.**

Your §10.3 mentions "B-tree and FTS5 indexes plus a flat vector matrix." The `sqlite-vec` project (cross-platform SQLite extension for vector search) is purpose-built for this use case and eliminates the need for a separate vector database dependency.【turn2search7】

For the knowledge graph itself, your SQLite schema design (§10.1) is sound. Add:
- **Hybrid search:** FTS5 BM25 scores fused with vector cosine similarity (this is what modern RAG does)
- **Structured extraction:** Store claim embeddings separately from full-text embeddings so users can query "show me claims about Ashokan pillars" semantically

**ObjectBox** is a strong alternative if your vector count exceeds ~10,000 or you need real-time updates, but at 50–70 POIs with 10–20 reference images each, SQLite-vec is simpler and more debuggable.【turn2search8】

## III. Strategic Recommendations

### 1. **Reposition as "The Trust Layer for Heritage AI"**

The Indian government is building AI-powered heritage search (Feb 2026 announcement). Google Arts & Culture exists. What none of them offer is **verifiable, offline, citation-grounded truth on a personal device**. This is your moat.

**Innovative differentiation:** Package TrueWay's signed pack format + claim-level provenance system as an **open standard** for heritage content distribution. If museums, universities, and tourism boards adopt your format, you become infrastructure, not just an app. This is the "PDF of heritage content" play.

### 2. **Build a "Heritage Twin" Pilot for 1 Site**

Choose **one** monument (Mahabodhi Temple is ideal — documented, photogenic, publicly accessible). Build a complete digital twin using:
- Photogrammetry or LiDAR scan (partner with IIT Patna or Nalanda University)
- Niantic VPS2 anchor placement
- Gemma 4 E2B-powered conversational guide
- Full claim-level citations

This becomes your **flagship demo** for partners, investors, and the ASI. One perfect experience > 50 mediocre ones.

### 3. **Prepare for the On-Device Multimodal Revolution**

Gemma 4 E2B changes the economics of on-device AI. Within 12–18 months, every mid-range Android phone will handle a multimodal model. Your architecture should:

- **Abstract the model layer:** Design a `ModelProvider` interface that can swap between MediaPipe, Gemma 3n E2B, Gemma 4 E2B, and future models based on device capability
- **Version embeddings with model versions:** Your §9 already hints at this; make it explicit — reference embeddings are tied to a specific encoder version, and packs must declare compatibility

### 4. **Leverage India's Policy Tailwinds**

The Culture Ministry's AI heritage initiative (Feb 2026) and Indian Culture Portal V2.0 (March 2026) signal government investment in exactly this space.【turn0search11】【turn0search14】 TrueWay should:
- Position for ASI partnership under "Adopt a Heritage 2.0"
- Explore integration with the National AI Mission's cultural-heritage AI track
- Use the government's data infrastructure (Inheritage SDK, Indian Culture Portal) as content sources
